# Azure Banner — Day 10: The Old Chain Baths Relay

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 10  
**Location:** Old Chain Baths ruins, Zazesspur; customs offices / The Low Arch

## Chronological log

- **Following Whitewater signals:** The day focused on locating a relay point associated with the stolen ledger and the suspected **Weigher**. The companions approached the ruined **Old Chain Baths** without treating the whole visible upper building as the true hideout.
- **Vesper's reconnaissance:** Edric's owl observed no obvious rooftop guard, but found recent movement and a new **rope ladder** descending to service rooms. Three people were active below: the **sling-woman who had escaped the Drowned Heron**, a slight adolescent courier, and an **older, lean man in dock clothes** apparently directing them. The lower room connected to an **eastern drain** and deeper cistern/service passages.
- **Coordinated approach:** **Pip** concealed himself by the **deeper exit**, while **Gaspar** and **Edric** approached from the eastern drain. This split positioned Pip to intercept someone trying to flee with evidence rather than forcing a frontal chase through the ruins.
- **The confrontation:** Edric cast *Sleep*, temporarily **Incapacitating the adolescent courier** but not the two adults. Gaspar readied *Ray of Frost* and struck the older man as he tried to escape with a **narrow case**, slowing him. At the choke point Pip ambushed the older man with a **shortsword** and knocked him unconscious **alive**, allowing the case to be secured. The three relay suspects were eventually captured alive.
- **Relay evidence:** The search produced a hidden cache/niche holding a **relay ledger, coded route slips, a brass scale-and-wave token, weights-and-measures dispatches, and a sealed packet marked 'W'**. The lower network had an unsecured deeper passage; a captured relay did not mean the entire underground route was cleared.
- **Custody and Mere's judgment:** The party brought the captives and evidence to **Deputy Tallymaster Orsana Mere**. She tied the scale-and-wave token to earlier seizures, ordered official record cross-checks, and kept the **'W' packet sealed** for witnessed opening. She judged the older prisoner a **coordinator**, not proven to be the Weigher, and took care to question the intimidated courier separately.
- **Official support and reward:** Mere paid **50 gp** for the operation and offered a **further contingent 25 gp** for identifying or capturing the Weigher. She arranged plainclothes observation around **The Low Arch** for two nights and suggested the inn's secured rear court for the party's animals, cart, and companions.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Shared purse after the 50 gp payment:** **481 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp**, before later lodging and operational expenses.
- The three Old Chain Baths relay captives are **alive in official custody**; the older man is **not** conclusively identified as the Weigher.
- The **25 gp contingency is not yet earned/received**. Whitewater signals and official measurements remain an open investigative thread.
