# Azure Banner — Day 12: Northward on Mavros's Trail

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 12  
**Location:** Zazesspur; north road toward the North Road Survey Depot

## Chronological log

- **Payment for the counting-house case:** Deputy Tallymaster **Orsana Mere** rewarded the party with **75 gp** for recovering the gray calfskin correction ledger, capturing **Marris Pell alive**, preserving the alterations, and showing the connection between Whitewater and official records.
- **A more serious assignment:** Mere issued **sealed customs credentials** and instructions to learn whether **Deputy Road Surveyor Aldren Mavros** had reached the **North Road Survey Depot**, near the southern approach to the Grim Jaws. This was a **separate investigation**, with compensation to be determined after verified findings on the party's return.
- **Preparation and economy:** **Pip** arranged modest road resupply—fresh food, extra horse feed, and small camp consumables—for **2 gp**. The party did not purchase a wagon, new draft horse, extra riding horse, or felling axe at this stage.
- **The full traveling company sets out:** **Gaspar, Pip, and Edric** departed Zazesspur shortly after midday with **Nib, Ripper, Vesper, Lucero, Castaña, and the modified cart**. They expected the depot to be about a day's practical travel away and did **not** plan to continue onward into the Grim Jaws.
- **An overnight stop:** Because departure was after midday, the party camped once on the road, intending to arrive at the depot the next morning. The camp was not a newly garrisoned safe rest site merely because the group erected a shelter.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Purse accounting:** 447 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp immediately before Mere's 75 gp payment; **522 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** afterward; **520 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** after the 2 gp road resupply.
- **Active assignment:** Determine Mavros's whereabouts and investigate interference with road-survey records; report back to Mere in Zazesspur.
- **Custody:** North Tally prisoners and documentary evidence remain with city authorities. The sealed credentials, not seized criminal evidence, are what the traveling party carries for official identification.
