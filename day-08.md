# Azure Banner — Day 08: A Working Day at The Low Arch

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 8 (after the interlude and the city's Day 7 jobs)  
**Location:** Zazesspur, chiefly The Low Arch and the Lower City  
**Party:** Gaspar Espada, Pip Underbough, Edric Vale; Nib, Ripper, and Vesper

## Chronological log

- **A quieter day after dangerous work:** After the Clack, Sunk Lantern, and Tavares & Bellan assignments, the group set aside time for repairs, practical outfitting, modest comforts, and consolidating its resources at **Halda Veyr's Low Arch**.
- **Edric and Gaspar's magic project:** Edric scribed a *Magic Missile* spell scroll, with **Gaspar assisting**. The scroll was subsequently **transferred to Gaspar**; it is one physical scroll, not an additional copy in Edric's belongings.
- **Pip looks after Nib:** Pip altered a reinforced dock coat for **Nib**, leaving him with a practical fitted protective garment (**AC 13**). Nib's gear is not interchangeable with Ripper's, and the two continued to require care when moving around a crowded city.
- **Small-group amenities:** The party acquired a **cedar storage box** to keep weapons and implements from lying loose, and a **Fox-and-Geese board and counters** for entertainment. Provisions, small supplies and the day's craft requirements added to the expense.
- **Nib and Ripper's supervised outing:** The companions brought Nib and the wolf out into parts of the Lower City under supervision, continuing to normalize their unusual travel arrangements rather than treating them as prisoners or disposable assets.
- **Accounting:** Recorded day expenses totaled **26 gp**, leaving **386 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** in the shared purse at the Day 8 close.

## Key interactions and choices

- **Edric / Gaspar:** Arcane instruction began to yield usable crafted resources rather than only battle spells.
- **Pip / Nib:** Armor alterations and careful storage illustrated Pip's role as treasurer and quartermaster; looking after the goblin and wolf was a deliberate responsibility.
- **Halda Veyr:** The Low Arch continued as the group's dependable base in Zazesspur.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- The next day's fresh assignment is the **stolen customs ledger**, not a continuation of the Day 7 dockside crate raid.
- **386 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** is the Day 8 purse balance. A later **5 gp healer's-kit purchase** reduces that figure to **381 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** before the Day 9 reward.
- Nib's fitted coat, the cedar box, Fox-and-Geese set, and Gaspar's single *Magic Missile* scroll are established possessions.
