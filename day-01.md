# Azure Banner — Day 01: The Road East

**In-world day:** 1  
**Location:** Road from Valespada toward Darromar, Tethyr  
**Party:** Gaspar Espada, Pip Underbough, Edric Vale; Lucero (Gaspar's riding horse) and Castaña (the cart horse)

## Chronological log

- **Departure context:** The trio left Valespada on **Night 0**, the night preceding this travel day. Gaspar, a younger son of House Espada, was striking out with his companions rather than being exiled; the cart and both horses were already with them.
- **First day on the road:** They made their way east/northeast toward Darromar. The journey was dusty and tiring but passed without combat or a hostile encounter. A roadside shrine offering, a simple meal under an oak, and practical attention to the horses and cart marked the day.
- **First camp:** They camped in a sheltered hollow, cooked beans, divided watch duty, and began establishing their characteristic blend of Gaspar's idealism, Pip's practical caution, and Edric's studious curiosity.
- **The sky omen:** During this initial travel/camping period, the travelers saw three striking blue-white lights in the sky; the lights separated, with one taking on a reddish westward course and another continuing north. The event made an impression, particularly on Gaspar. **The omen is not established as the same phenomenon as the blue lantern later discovered at the tollhouse.**

## End-of-day state / continuity

- The companions were still on the road to Darromar, with their equipment, cart, Lucero, and Castaña.
- No jobs had been accepted, no goblin group had been encountered, and no connection to the later Black Antler trouble had yet been proved.
- **Date guardrail:** The departure itself and earliest sighting are sometimes narrated together with Day 1, but the campaign ledger explicitly calls the departure **Night 0**.
