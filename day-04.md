# Azure Banner — Day 04: The Corza Farm and Blue Tollhouse

**In-world day:** 4  
**Location:** Corza farm northwest of Darromar; abandoned old tollhouse

## Chronological log

- **Farm inquiry:** The trio visited **Matten Corza** to investigate missing sheep. They heard about a mutilated ewe, the daughter's sighting of blue light, and the nearby disused tollhouse. Corza's daughter **Lysa** and the farm dog **Bran** were part of the household setting. An early search of the tracks was inconclusive; Pip's low Investigation result did not solve the mystery at the farm.
- **Property respected:** A concealed emergency cache beneath Corza's floorboards was **left untouched**. Its contents were not fully confirmed and never became party loot.
- **Tollhouse discovery:** At the old tollhouse they found **Marda** (wounded, formerly the Black Antlers' cook) and **Skit**, sheltering with a human infant they called **Little Bird**. The child was later identified as **Liora Tessen**. The apparent abduction proved to be a rescue: Marda, Skit, and Marda's partner **Zumo** had fled the Black Antlers rather than allow the baby to be harmed. Zumo had died protecting their escape.
- **Explaining the oddities:** The eerie glow came from an old, weakly enchanted **blue-glass toll lantern** placed in an upper window, not evidence that the goblins had caused the sky omen. The refugees admitted taking sheep for survival, while the violence done to an ewe was connected to a wolf.
- **Wolf in the cellar:** Pip helped calm and release **Ripper** without fighting him, using sheep meat and a route out through the culvert. This became important to the wolf's later relationship with the goblin **Nib**.
- **Choice of mercy:** Gaspar, Pip, and Edric decided to protect the fugitives and child rather than hand them over reflexively as kidnappers. Pip helped work out arrangements and supplies; the others investigated the cellar and the site.
- **Night defense:** They kept the tollhouse occupied and guarded through the night, aware that the Black Antlers might come looking.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Protected:** Marda, Skit, and infant Little Bird/Liora.
- **Threat:** The Black Antlers still held or endangered other Tessen family members; their leaders remained at large.
- **Next:** Bruk One-Tine's search party and the urgent evacuation unfold on **Day 5**.
