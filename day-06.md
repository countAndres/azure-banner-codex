# Azure Banner — Day 06: Rescue of the Tessen Family

**In-world day:** 6  
**Location:** Darromar; Vaelorn westfields/Burner's Hollow rescue area; return to Darromar

## Chronological log

- **Morning preparations:** Following a safe rest, the trio continued their level-2 development and prepared to join **House Vaelorn's third muster**. The first two sorties had already left. They left **Castaña and the cart** at the Hall of the Even Hand; the refugee goblins remained in its care. **Edric** had **Ice Knife** ready for field use. He had *Find Familiar* in his spellbook but initially still needed incense.
- **Joining the rescue:** On the road the new muster encountered riders associated with the earlier Vaelorn efforts and consolidated warnings about the Black Antlers and their captives. The party and Vaelorn forces prioritized finding the living prisoners, not conducting a reckless pursuit of the enemy leaders.
- **The rescue action:** During the operation around **Burner's Hollow** and the captives' relocation route, **Pip** identified dangers and freed **Harl Tessen**; **Gaspar** protected allies at close quarters and used his developing magical abilities; **Edric** contributed spells including *Ice Knife*, *Magic Missile*, and *Mage Hand*. **Bela Tessen** and **Harl** were recovered alive from the relocation danger, and **Darron Tessen** was recovered at Burner's Hollow. Infant **Liora** had already been saved with Marda on Day 4; this day's achievement included the family's survival and reunion, not a second independent rescue of the baby.
- **Goblin consequences:** The captor **Tikkit** was taken alive. **Kesh Grey-Ear**, a goblin who tried to help by cutting a mule's trace during the crisis, was killed by Tikkit. Kesh's role mattered to judging the goblins as individuals rather than one undifferentiated enemy faction.
- **Return to Darromar:** The party and survivors came back in the later day. **Bela**, reassured that Liora was alive, came to acknowledge that **Marda** had saved her daughter, changing her feelings toward the goblin refugee. **Corporal Marrón** questioned Tikkit while the companions reported and sought further aid.
- **New allies introduced:** At Lady **Amara Vaelorn's** court, the trio met **Seraphine Valoris**, a dragonborn Tyrran paladin accompanied by two knight initiates, and **Thalion Riversong**, a half-elf ranger engaged to track the Black Antlers. They were prospective allies in the pursuit, **not permanent party members**.
- **Warning and night reconnaissance:** Information about **Ragha Blackhorn's** vengeance suggested that the Black Antlers might move toward **Valespada**. Gaspar wrote a warning and sought a fast messenger; Pip addressed expenses and reimbursement with Vaelorn. Edric obtained the needed incense, summoned his owl familiar **Vesper**, and sent her scouting west-southwest. She detected broad movement but no pinpointed camp. The party took a **Safe Long Rest** in Darromar.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Survivors:** Harl, Bela, Darron, and Liora Tessen alive; Marda and Skit still sheltered under protection.
- **Captured:** Tikkit. **Still at large:** Ragha and Bruk.
- **Next:** A fresh Vaelorn/Tyrran pursuit sets out on **Day 7**.
