# Azure Banner — Day 07: Pursuit to Bellwether Fold

**In-world day:** 7  
**Location:** Southwest of Darromar, toward Valespada and Bellwether Fold; evening return to Darromar

## Chronological log

- **The pursuit begins:** After their Darromar Safe Long Rest, **Gaspar**, **Pip**, and **Edric** joined a Vaelorn force under **Captain Dain**, with **Seraphine Valoris**, two fellow Tyrran initiates, and ranger **Thalion Riversong**. The trio operated with the forward scouts while the main force stayed within supporting distance. Vesper could carry a warning or guide troops toward a discovery.
- **Finding the trail:** Clues from the Black Antler prisoners, charcoal/farm tracks, and Vesper's scouting led the force southwest. The scouts quietly overcame a **five-goblin warning post**, preserved the silence, and sent Vesper back to guide Dain and Seraphine to secure the captives. **Bruk's smoke trap** later alerted watchers and prompted the main column to close up.
- **Defeating an ambush:** Vesper spotted a rear-guard trap in a sunken lane. **Pip** crept forward and cut/fouled the **trigger cord**, preventing the opening trap from firing. Vaelorn and Tyrran troops were able to turn the encounter against the Black Antler rear guard; multiple goblins were subdued or captured. Edric used *Fire Bolt* during the fighting.
- **Bellwether Fold confrontation:** The force closed on **Ragha Blackhorn** and **Bruk One-Tine**. **Edric's Ice Knife** disrupted the defenders; Pip's archery and Edric's *Magic Missile* helped bring **Bruk down alive**. **Gaspar** held the shield line with Seraphine and the Vaelorn fighters, used his swordsmanship and Action Surge, and interposed his shield during the clash. **Ragha was captured alive**, breaking the local Black Antler command.
- **Protecting the tenants:** **Pip and Thalion** scouted west toward Valespada. They found the **Bellwether tenant cart and mule unharmed** and spotted a goblin runner positioned to threaten them. Pip stopped an immediate attack and persuaded the runner to surrender by telling him that Ragha, Bruk, and Tikkit had fallen. Gaspar remained with the larger force and prisoners; **two riders carried the warning farther west** toward Valespada.
- **Return and official custody:** That evening the column reached Darromar. **Deputy-Marshal Orven Tal** of the Royal Constabulary—dry and businesslike, but competent—took Ragha, Bruk, the runner, and the other Black Antler captives into custody. Captain Dain and his troops were relieved to relinquish prisoner duty. **Lady Amara Vaelorn** provided reimbursement and asked the trio to return the next morning to discuss the captives' disposition.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Black Antler leaders Ragha and Bruk are alive in Crown custody**, not dead or roaming free. The westward runner was captured; the Bellwether tenants survived.
- **Marda and Skit** were at the Hall of the Even Hand with the Tessens. **Tikkit and Vek** had passed into Constabulary custody; **Nib and Ripper** were also temporarily held with arrangements to keep them together.
- **Day 8 handoff:** A discussion of the goblins' future, subsequent level advancement, and onward plans comes **after** this day, not during the Day 7 pursuit.
