# Azure Banner — Day 02: Three Reeds

**In-world day:** 2  
**Location:** Darromar outskirts; the Three Reeds inn

## Chronological log

- **Approach to Darromar:** Continuing from the Valespada road, Gaspar, Pip, and Edric reached the outskirts of Darromar with Lucero, Castaña, and their cart.
- **Choosing shelter:** The group weighed another field camp against staying at **Three Reeds**. A coin toss helped settle the choice in favor of the inn, run by **Mariela Tann**. They arranged supper, stabling, and modest accommodation: two beds and a pallet.
- **First lead:** They encountered or learned the account of **Tomas Rell**, an outrider associated with the **Aldana Merchant Coster**. Rell's report concerned the Corza farm: sheep missing, an ewe mutilated, uncanny blue lights around an abandoned tollhouse, and scraping or whispering sounds. The report pointed them toward Darromar's **Southbank Watch** for further information.
- **Decision:** Rather than immediately charging off after strange lights, they treated the report as a lead to verify when looking for work in the city. They spent the night at Three Reeds.

## Key NPCs

- **Mariela Tann:** Innkeeper at Three Reeds; a useful initial contact on Darromar's outskirts.
- **Tomas Rell:** Merchant-coster outrider whose observations brought the Corza/tollhouse mystery to the trio's attention.
- **Matten Corza:** Farm owner named in the developing lead. Some early retellings say **Mateo**; do not invent a second Corza farmer from this variant.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- The party had **heard** the Corza report, not yet investigated the farm or found the goblin refugees.
- The fuller Darromar job search and the Watch's official offer occur on **Day 3**, not this day.
