# Azure Banner — Day 11: The North Tally Corrections

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 11  
**Location:** North bonded-court district, North Tally complex, covered counting-house yard; Zazesspur customs

## Chronological log

- **An official-records lead:** Deputy Tallymaster **Orsana Mere** tasked the trio with following irregular Whitewater-related transfer activity in the **North Tally** area. A collector had replaced a north-pan dead-drop packet with a **black-corner-notched** one; **Pip** watched the **Halren & Voss** vicinity, **Vesper** providing overhead coverage while Gaspar and Edric remained ready nearby.
- **An older visitor followed:** Pip trailed a suspicious older visitor **without being noticed**. Vesper saw the man pass through the **public North Tally gate**, but lost him under the covered buildings. The party did **not** learn which inner office he entered and sensibly avoided a reckless pursuit into uncertain rooms.
- **Missing correction ledger:** Mere learned that **Marris Pell**, a senior clerk associated with corrections, was absent and the **gray calfskin correction ledger** was missing. The investigation followed the movement of a ledger **north by foot, then by handcart** into the covered counting-house yard.
- **Close observation:** Pip moved unseen to a storage room's only practical door while **Vesper** spotted the missing ledger on a worktable through the high louver. **Marris Pell** was inside, apparently nervous, actively **scraping, cutting, replacing and resealing selected folios** using a knife, scraper, replacement pages, wax, and an oil lamp. The barred clerestory was too small to escape through; a thin watcher lingered near the copying office outside.
- **Intervention:** The trio moved to preserve the physical proof rather than allow the book to be destroyed or the falsification finished. **Pip captured Marris alive**; **Edric extinguished fire** before it could ruin the record. The original volume, replacement/removed leaves, tools, thread, wax, and notes were preserved and secured by Mere's office.
- **The significance:** Altered official weights-and-measures/record corrections connected Whitewater to institutional record manipulation, not only tavern kidnappings or street theft. **Marris's live capture** provided another person the authorities could question.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Marris Pell is alive and in custody**; the original **gray calfskin ledger and alteration evidence** belong to the investigation, not party gear.
- The **75 gp reward** for the North Tally/Marris operation is **not paid until Day 12**; do not credit it here.
- The Weigher's precise identity and command structure remain unresolved. A missing visitor's route beyond the **public North Tally gate** was never established.
- The trio's advancement toward their **confirmed level-4** capabilities is tied to the recent successes; any individual numerical XP ledger is interpretive alongside milestone-led leveling.
