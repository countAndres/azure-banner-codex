# Azure Banner — Interlude: Darromar to Zazesspur

**Coverage:** After the original Darromar Day 07 pursuit and before the later Zazesspur Day 08. This is a **multi-day bridge, not an additional single-day entry**.

## Chronological bridge

- **Darromar aftermath:** Following Ragha Blackhorn and Bruk One-Tine's capture, Deputy-Marshal **Orven Tal** took custody of the Black Antler prisoners. **Lady Amara Vaelorn** requested a further meeting about the captive goblins. **Marda and Skit** remained with the Tessens at the Hall of the Even Hand; **Tikkit and Vek** were moved into Constabulary custody. **Nib and his wolf Ripper**, briefly under special custody arrangements, later rejoined the trio.
- **Toward a new destination:** The party planned westbound travel from **Darromar to Zazesspur**, spending additional time arranging passage with a caravan rather than setting out instantly after the Bellwether fighting. They took trade wine for resale and kept their cart, **Castaña**, and Gaspar's riding horse **Lucero**.
- **Caravan leadership:** **Captain Maerla Sorn** commanded the westbound merchant caravan. She was competent and suspicious of bandit infiltrators: after a passenger disappeared overnight, she logged the absence and warned her guards, but did not promptly inform the trio, partly because their separately placed camp with a goblin and wolf might draw attention from raiders.
- **Road companionship and magic:** Traveling mage **Ilvarren Thale**, who appreciated good wine and interesting stories, taught the three companions after hearing how they came to travel with Nib and Ripper. His familiar **Aurel** was a condor (not Edric's owl **Vesper**). He accepted wine worth about **5 gp** in exchange; he also provided Pip a **spell component pouch** and later notes on component suppliers. The tutoring explains Gaspar's Eldritch Knight, Pip's Arcane Trickster, and Edric's Evoker development. At a caravan gathering, **Edric danced with guard Talla Greyhook**; bard **Lysaer Reed** was among the travelers.
- **Road threat:** Farms and familiar company became sparse as the caravan traveled west. Bandits attempted to isolate or disable a vehicle or two and seize goods or people the caravan left behind; they were not strong enough to conquer the entire procession. The company eventually reached Zazesspur, having camped outside its closed gates before entering the following morning. **Ilvarren and Thalion Riversong** did not become permanent members of the traveling party.
- **Zazesspur lodgings:** The party established itself at **The Low Arch**, kept by **Halda Veyr**, with practical arrangements for Nib and Ripper. Their first night at the inn cost **4 gp**.
- **Zazesspur Day 07 — Clack drainage-lane bounty:** The trio chose a drainage-lane job involving **Clack** and a carrion crawler. **Gaspar** held the creature in melee, **Pip** fired on it from behind Gaspar's line, and **Edric** used *Magic Missile*. The mission resulted in a rescue and improved their standing locally.
- **Zazesspur Day 07 — missing muleteers:** Pip sold the remaining trade wine for **55 gp net**. Meanwhile Gaspar and Edric traced vanished teamsters **Darrik Voss** and **Hema Lorn** from **The Three Stubborn Saints** to **The Sunk Lantern**, a sinister under-arch tavern. Pip found a gray-capped porter with distinctive bad boots and a wet service entrance, then concealed himself to observe the lower cellar. The trio breached the cellar, rescued both muleteers alive and captured keeper **Harl**, the porter, a serving woman, and a fourth accomplice alive. Pip and Edric found a concealed victim/cargo ledger, intact contents of an opened leather tube, stolen valuables, a drugging draught, and a back drain route. **Ravella Sornhook** confirmed the cargo and survivors and paid the **40 gp** rescue reward; evidence and stolen property passed to the authorities. The letter **“W”** remained a clue.
- **Zazesspur Day 07 — Tavares & Bellan dockside job:** The party investigated a missing long crate at a bonded cellar. Pip discovered that it had gone **downward through a concealed tide-hatch**, not out the locked gate: disturbed dust, cold salt/tar air, and rope/hoist marks revealed a waterborne smuggling route to a canal skiff. The crate was recovered intact; **three thieves** were taken alive, and clues included **Whitewater/W** and **Three Gull Stairs**. The agreed dockside fee totaled **185 gp** across method, route, crate, proof, and discretion conditions.

## Continuity / uncertainty

- **Day-numbering:** The later `day-08.md` is Zazesspur Day 08, **not the immediate successor day to Darromar Day 07**. The intervening span above must remain visible in any consolidated timeline.
- **Ownership:** Rescued muleteers' cargo, Sunk Lantern stolen property, and the seized bonded-crate evidence are not freely retained party loot.
- **Party roster:** The regular party is Gaspar, Pip, Edric, with Nib and Ripper as companions. The caravan associates and Tyrran/Vaelorn allies are not permanent PCs.
