# Azure Banner — Day 13: The Survey Depot and Chain Hut

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 13  
**Location:** North Road Survey Depot; drainage hollow and abandoned chain hut to the south

## Chronological log

- **Arrival and alarm:** The trio arrived at the **North Road Survey Depot** and presented **Mere's sealed customs credentials**. **Aldren Mavros had not reached his expected destination**. His work area and surveying records offered leads suggesting foul play rather than an innocent travel delay.
- **The hidden carriage:** Depot rider **Lysa Tor** guided the party to a **screened off-road drainage hollow south of the depot** where Mavros's survey carriage had been found. The site would not have been visible from the main road. The party deliberately left **Castaña, Lucero, the cart, Nib, and Ripper** at the depot rather than trampling the clues or burdening their stealth; **Vesper** joined the search.
- **Tracing the abductors:** Clues led to an **abandoned chain hut**. Vesper reconnoitered and found a **concealed mule in a rear lean-to**, a rough **alarm cord** across the approach, and a **rear shutter/escape opening**. There were two visible captors: a paperwork-oriented chainman/survey-clerk type and a heavier guard. **Mavros was alive**, bound, hurt, and exhausted inside.
- **Silent positioning and rescue:** **Pip** took a concealed lookout position, while the others maneuvered to prevent an alarm or escape. The trio overcame the two captors and **rescued Mavros alive**, preserving him as a witness instead of accepting a mere report of what happened.
- **The forgery and altered measurements:** Mavros identified the chainman as **Calvern Rusk**. The heavier accomplice's personal name has not been established. The party preserved **modified surveying chain/equipment**, forged permission, route documents, seals/wax and stamps, and a **transfer note**. Together these suggested an attempt to corrupt route data and official authorization rather than simply rob a traveler.
- **Safe regrouping:** The party returned to the depot with **Mavros and two living captives**. The witness was debriefed; arrangements were made to protect and divide crucial documents in transit rather than trust a single copy or satchel. **Lysa Tor** would accompany the return to Zazesspur.
- **Full rest at the depot:** The secured depot counted as a **Safe Long Rest**, restoring the party's usual resources before the next day's potentially dangerous return.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Rescued:** Aldren Mavros, alive, a significant witness. **Captured:** Calvern Rusk and an unnamed heavy guard, alive.
- The modified chain and forged papers are investigative evidence, not acquired adventuring gear.
- **Next:** Return south to Zazesspur on Day 14 with Mavros, captives, Lysa, companions, and divided evidence. Whitewater agents may still be watching the road.
