# Azure Banner — Day 05: Bruk's Search Party and the Flight to Darromar

**In-world day:** 5  
**Location:** Old tollhouse and Corza country; road into Darromar; Hall of the Even Hand

## Chronological log

- **The pursuers arrive:** A Black Antler search party under **Bruk One-Tine** pressed the tollhouse. It included goblins **Nib** and **Vek** and a trained wolf, **Ripper**. **Skit** spotted the attempt to use the culvert and fought to defend the refugees. His arrow incapacitated **Vek**; Bruk eventually withdrew rather than being captured this day.
- **Avoiding unnecessary killing:** **Nib surrendered** and warned that more attackers and fire could follow. Nib's calls helped control or calm Ripper. The party stabilized Vek and secured him as a prisoner while making protection of the wounded Marda and the infant a priority.
- **Evacuating people, not merely winning a fight:** The group gathered the refugees, prisoners, Ripper, and the threatened **Corza household** for a move toward the safety of Darromar. A rescued ewe was also among the awkward practical burdens of the retreat. Bruk employed misleading calls and road hazards; **Pip** recognized and defeated obstacles rather than allowing a disorderly chase.
- **Safe entry:** The party obtained help from Darromar's Watch at the **west gate**, with **Corporal Ysabel Marrón** important to the handoff. They secured admission and protection rather than abandoning the goblins or infant outside the walls.
- **Sanctuary:** At the **Hall of the Even Hand**, Tyrran caretakers received Marda and Little Bird for care; Skit stayed nearby. **Nib and Ripper** were kept together under supervision in the stable court; **Vek** was held separately. The party gave testimony concerning the Black Antlers and the missing Tessen family.
- **Escalation:** Gaspar sought aid from **Lady Amara Vaelorn**. The appeal helped set House Vaelorn's westfields rescue forces in motion. The party rested in Darromar as the rescue musters gathered.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- The trio had saved the tollhouse refugees and brought them to shelter; **Harl, Bela, and Darron Tessen still needed rescuing**.
- **Bruk escaped the Day 5 engagement**. His eventual capture belongs to **Day 7**.
- The tollhouse was a possible future Vaelorn rendezvous/strongpost, **not yet an established permanently garrisoned safe-rest post**.
