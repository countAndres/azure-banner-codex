# Azure Banner — Days 08–14 (Zazesspur ledger)

These are **concise, chronological roleplaying-campaign logs**, meant for use alongside `day-01.md` through `day-07.md`, `scraps.md`, and the end-of-Day-14 JSON snapshot. They describe established events, not a transcript.

## Important chronological qualification

The original **Days 01–07** files document the **initial Darromar/Black Antler sequence**, ending with the Bellwether Fold pursuit on its Day 7. **The Zazesspur arc subsequently uses its own day numbering**, following intervening Darromar business, caravan arrangements, a multi-day westbound journey, and early Zazesspur activity. Thus **Zazesspur Day 08 is not literally the morning immediately after Darromar Day 07**. Do not silently merge the two clocks or teleport the party from Darromar to Zazesspur overnight.

Read `between-darromar-and-zazesspur.md` to bridge that interval (including the Zazesspur Day 07 quests). `day-08.md` through `day-14.md` then follow **the Zazesspur-local day count** used in later play. If consolidating them into a single absolute calendar in a future memory system, reconcile the dated travel-day ledger before assigning unique global day numbers.

At the end of Zazesspur Day 14, the party is at **The Low Arch**; Mavros and six captives have been handed to officials. The latest **confirmed**, not newly audited, common purse is **520 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp**. The Mavros reward has not yet been paid. The following morning's Hall of Measures meeting is **not** a Day 14 event.
