Azure Banner — Scraps and Small Continuity Details
Coverage: Campaign history through the end of in-world Day 14, after the return to Zazesspur. Do not interpret this file as events occurring after that point.
Purpose: Preserve easy-to-lose color, small props, personal obligations, NPC mannerisms, minor clues, and future seeds. The structured Day 14 JSON snapshot remains the authority for the party's main location, quest status, and shared purse.
Reading convention: Unless a bullet says Open, Proposed, Reserved, or Unverified, it refers to something already established in campaign play or character history. These labels matter: never turn an idea into an accomplished event just because it appears here.
Characters, family history, and recurring behavior
Gaspar Espada is the fourth son of a relatively minor noble family of Tethyr. He left comfortable Valespada voluntarily, pursuing a more adventurous future rather than being driven out.
House Espada descends from immigrant swordsmiths whose expertly made arming swords, and skill in using and teaching their use, helped earn the family's ennoblement.
Gaspar's ancestral blade is an older, rather short and awkward arming-sword pattern; exceptional craftsmanship compensates for its unfashionable dimensions. It is not inherently magical.
The sword retains ancestral blade, guard, and pommel, but its grip and scabbard have been replaced over time. It bears the old Espada maker's mark and could be recognized by knowledgeable smiths, historians, or Tethyrian nobility.
Open / future possibility: Restoration, naming, or enchanting the ancestral sword could become a meaningful craft project; no completed enchantment has been established.
Gaspar carries the House Espada signet/seal, fine clothes, an annotated magical-study book with handwritten formulae, and a small supply of experimental magic materials despite being a frontline swordsman.
Gaspar's shield bears, or is suitable to bear, House Espada's heraldic device.
Lucero, Gaspar's personal family-gifted riding horse, is a dark bay gelding with a white star. He is not automatically a spare riding horse for the rest of the company.
Castaña, the group's draft horse, is a patient chestnut mare. She is the cart's working animal, not merely scenery.
Pip Underbough is the practical quartermaster and treasurer. He thinks in supplies, resale values, marginal expenses, carrying capacity, and whether an aristocratic promise will actually cover costs.
A recurring comic contrast: Gaspar is the idealist inclined to omens and bold gestures; Pip remembers the price, weight, receipt, and repercussions.
Pip is the person most likely to know where the rope, candles, bookkeeping paper, spare gear, or a little brandy can be found.
Pip's recurring ledger humor includes the sentiment that his ledger may be damaged but not defeated. He is particularly alert to noble assurances of what would be "fair."
Edric Vale is scholarly, cautious, and increasingly obliged to learn magic in field conditions, not the peaceful setting he might have preferred.
Edric's recurrent comic contrast is between book learning and the physically unpleasant, improvised realities of traveling and fighting.
The group's older recurring conversational motifs include "both can be true," "not yet," and "the wall began it"; keep these as callbacks, not compulsory catchphrases or proof that any single character said them on every occasion.
Vesper is Edric's owl familiar and useful airborne scout. Earlier, Edric had Find Familiar in his spellbook but lacked its incense; that was an earlier obstacle, not an outstanding Day 14 inability to have a familiar.
Pip and Edric do not simply inherit all of Gaspar's highborn assumptions about danger, obligation, or money. Their different instincts are part of the trio's tone.
World/roster intention: Gaspar's ambitions will eventually contribute to founding the Azure Banner; the mercenary company itself is not yet an accomplished in-story Day 14 institution.
Valespada, Darromar, and the Black Antler aftermath
Geography: Valespada is roughly 25 miles west-southwest of Darromar. The journey from Darromar toward Zazesspur is westbound; do not casually switch it to southbound.
The first adventure grew out of the Corza/Tessen rescue, the goblin refugees, and the danger presented by Ragha, Bruk, and the Black Antlers.
Marda had been the Black Antlers' cook. She fled with her partner Zumo and Skit after the band demanded an atrocity involving a captive human infant. Zumo was killed; Marda was injured.
Kesh Grey-Ear is a name from the goblin/Black Antler continuity worth retaining rather than replacing with a generic goblin name.
The threatened child and the clue/name "Little Bird" (Liora) are part of the rescue history. Do not forget that the bandit/goblin conflict involved actual families rather than anonymous quest markers.
Skit was given back three recoverable Black Antler arrows, then seven more from Pip's quiver, reaching 12 arrows at that point; Pip was left with 13. These are historical counts, not guaranteed Day 14 quiver totals.
The Corza farm has a concealed under-floorboard household cache: small savings and personal/property papers or keepsakes. It was left untouched at the farm, not put in the party's inventory and not claimed as adventuring loot. The exact contents were not fully verified.
The Hall of the Even Hand was a place of shelter, care, and trusted safekeeping for rescued people and goblin refugees, not merely a quest hand-in point.
After the Black Antler arrests, Marda and Skit stayed with the Tessen family at the Hall of the Even Hand.
Tikkit and Vek were transferred to the Royal Constabulary. Nib was also temporarily placed in official custody, with special consideration to let him remain close to Ripper; later events confirm Nib and Ripper traveled with the party again.
Nib and Ripper are a bond, not two interchangeable followers. Nib's attempts to manage the wolf in cramped or social circumstances can be a character moment.
Lady Amara Vaelorn is an important early patron; the party's rescue efforts earned a hearing, help, and a basis for future trust.
Corporal Marrón handled or helped with Black Antler interrogation at the Hall while Gaspar and Pip dealt with Lady Amara; Edric's subsequent findings mattered to the next decision.
Thalion Riversong is the ranger's more consistently established name; one older note calls him "Riverson." Treat that as a continuity/spelling discrepancy, not as two separate rangers.
Seraphine Valoris is a dragonborn Tyrran knight-figure introduced with two other initiates in Lady Amara's company. She has been met, but has not simply been absorbed into the traveling trio as a permanent member.
Ragha's anger and desire for revenge helped motivate the fear that the Black Antlers might threaten Valespada.
Gaspar wrote a warning letter to Valespada and sought a fast messenger. Open: A definite account of delivery, family reaction, or later reply is not established in the available record.
On the pursuit, Pip quietly disabled the sunken-lane ambush's trigger cord before the enemy could spring it; this let the Vaelorn/Tyrran force counter the trap.
A smoke trap on Bruk's route compromised the pursuit's complete stealth, encouraging the main force to close its distance from the scouts.
West of Bellwether, Pip and Thalion found living Bellwether tenants and a goblin runner who might have endangered the cart or mule; Pip won the runner's surrender by telling him Ragha, Bruk, and Tikkit had fallen.
Deputy-Marshal Orven Tal, a competent but rather dry and somewhat disinterested Royal Constabulary official, accepted custody of Black Antler prisoners. His bureaucratic tone is a useful contrast to the rescuers' urgency.
Captain Dain and the Vaelorn soldiers were understandably relieved to hand their prisoners over and return to ordinary, relatively safe city duty.
The old tollhouse/strongpost was a potential refuge, but it only qualifies for an unrestricted Safe Long Rest when actually secured and garrisoned. The memory of the early blue lantern, wolf, sheep theft, and improvised defense belongs to that setting.
Caravan west and memorable road company
Captain Maerla Sorn commanded the caravan toward Zazesspur. She had her own rules, manifest, suspicions, and security priorities rather than functioning as a party-controlled escort.
Maerla recorded a passenger's suspicious nighttime disappearance in her ledger and alerted her guards. She did not initially tell the trio, partly because she hoped their separate camp with Nib and Ripper could draw or expose a raid.
The trio, Nib, and Ripper traveled in a relatively separate position from the caravan's main body and were responsible for much of their own defense and supplies.
Ilvarren Thale, an older mage, favored decent wine and interesting company. He offered arcane tutoring in exchange for some trade wine and the trio's story of how they came to travel with a goblin and a wolf.
Ilvarren's familiar Aurel is a condor; do not confuse Aurel with Edric's owl Vesper.
Ilvarren's share of the wine ultimately reduced its expected trade value by 5 gp. The bargain mattered more for Gaspar, Pip, and Edric's new arcane capabilities than for its beverage economics.
Ilvarren helped set the stage for Eldritch Knight Gaspar, Arcane Trickster Pip, and Evoker Edric during their level-3 development; that tutoring is an in-world explanation for the subclasses, not an unearned sudden ability.
Pip was a newly trained spellcaster and had not casually been using spells all along; his earliest Arcane Trickster progress involved genuine first attempts and practice.
Ilvarren gave Pip a component pouch and, upon parting, useful notes on spell-component suppliers. Do not silently give Pip a second, previously unrecorded arcane focus.
Caravan bard Lysaer Reed is a named minor road companion to preserve.
Edric danced competently with guard Talla Greyhook at the caravan gathering — a small, unexpectedly humanizing moment that need not be treated as a permanent romance.
The journey's mood changed gradually: friendly holdings and travelers dwindled, giving way to abandoned stops, worn mills, rougher travelers, and a less certain road.
The bandits on the road were not a force capable of destroying the entire caravan. Their plan was to immobilize or isolate one or two vehicles and take whatever cargo or prisoners the convoy left behind.
Ilvarren and Thalion did not become permanent members of the Zazesspur party; they went their separate ways after arrival.
Small Zazesspur possessions, accommodations, and street-level texture
Mistress Halda Veyr is the proprietor of The Low Arch, the party's recurring inn in Zazesspur.
The party initially paid 4 gp for the first night at The Low Arch; an accommodation arrangement was made for Nib and Ripper, including cellar space.
Pip sold the remaining trade wine for 55 gp net. This was a respectable recovery of its value, not an unexpected windfall or a new merchant friendship.
In the Clack/drainage-lane bounty, Gaspar held the carrion crawler in the narrow front while Pip withdrew enough to fire and Edric used Magic Missile. The creature recoiled toward the settling room under their attack.
A successful rescue in the Clack gave the party slightly more favorable local reception before they sought to sell the wine.
Ravella Sornhook became a useful named contact in the missing-muleteers work; she verified the recovered cargo and the rescued team.
The missing muleteers were Darrik Voss and Hema Lorn. Their mules and ordinary cargo had remained at The Three Stubborn Saints, a clue they had probably not left freely.
The Sunk Lantern was an under-arch tavern with a grimy watcher slit, discreet service access, a wet carry-track, and a concealed lower cellar route.
The Sunk Lantern's gray-capped porter was distinguished by bad boots and a foul-smelling strapped bucket. Pip concealed himself near the service door and overheard the porter hiss "Quiet" into the lower level.
The Sunk Lantern tavern keeper was Harl. The keeper, gray-capped porter, serving woman, and fourth associate were all taken alive; Edric's Sleep and the trio's use of nonlethal force mattered.
Search of the cellar turned up a concealed victim/cargo ledger, the muleteers' opened leather tube and intact contents, stolen valuables, a sleeping draught, the disposal bucket, and a hidden back drain-door used for moving captives.
The ledger, stolen goods, leather tube, and incriminating objects were treated as evidence and returned property, not treasure the trio kept.
The letter "W" linked the Sunk Lantern matter to a wider line of investigation; it was not resolved by simply arresting Harl.
At Tavares & Bellan, an apparently locked bonded cellar concealed an old tide-hatch/flood-service panel underneath the missing long crate's dust outline. Rope and hoist marks, salt/tar draft, and falling dust revealed how cargo bypassed the locked gate.
After the Tavares matter, ordinary confiscated or recovered low-value gear was released: reinforced dock coat, belt knives, cudgel, cord, gloves, and a pry hook. The particularly relevant hidden knife/evidence was retained by authorities; verify where the released mundane pieces are stowed before treating each as personally carried.
Minor equipment scrap: Nib's coat was altered to provide AC 13; this is a previously recorded gear improvement worth carrying forward.
Minor camp/social scrap: A Fox-and-Geese board and counters were added to the group's ordinary recreational possessions.
Minor storage scrap: Weapons were organized in a cedar box with a notion of emergency access, rather than being assumed to lie loose where Nib or Ripper sleeps. Exact box contents can be checked on a future inventory audit.
Gaspar ultimately forged a second shortsword for Pip. Pip now fights with paired shortswords in melee; the prior "forge Pip another blade" promise is fulfilled, not an outstanding order.
A Magic Missile scroll was transferred from Edric to Gaspar. It is Gaspar's portable spell resource, not duplicated on Edric's list.
Gaspar and Edric discussed the much greater task of magically improving the ancestral sword: a proposed +1 enchantment would require substantial arcane expertise and an approximate 10-day / 200 gp materials undertaking. Proposed, not begun or completed.
Whitewater: easily misplaced clues and people (Days 9–11)
Taman Rill's slashed satchel suggested a rehearsed theft team, not a random cutpurse: basket-drop distraction, red-scarfed elbow control, a small right-handed cutter working at the left hip, and a probable watcher.
A length of black waxed thread smelling of clove oil and chalk/lime connected the theft to the red-scarfed porter, white dust on a veiled woman's glove, and the Whitewater method of marking things.
Marra's broth stall and the lower fish-stairs were meaningful street-level waypoints in the stolen-ledger investigation.
Noll, frightened and apt to flee, responded to Pip's reassurance and became more cooperative when promised protection.
Brant was identifiable by a rust/red scarf, dark beard, gold tooth, and clove smell; he was later subdued and taken into customs/Watch custody.
Miv is the red-haired young cutter involved in the satchel theft. She was frightened, and telling her Noll was safe was a humane, strategically important means of gaining her trust.
The veiled woman, the escaped sling-woman, and the recurring signals/phrases belong to the Whitewater web; capture of some operatives did not close every route or identify every superior.
A code phrase worth preserving verbatim is "Bell before green." Other leads included a Weigher and the Bellmark/Copperhook Bend–Limekiln Yard material.
At the Drowned Heron, Pip interfered with a rooftop signal cord. When the handler pulled it, the jam bought a short but valuable delay.
Pip attempted Minor Illusion in Brant's voice to fool the Drowned Heron handler, but the imitation was unconvincing; do not rewrite this as a successful deception.
Edric's fire and Pip's melee strikes helped bring down the Drowned Heron defenders; Pip specifically rendered a burned rear crossbow guard unconscious alive rather than killing him.
At the end of the Drowned Heron fight, the original customs ledger, copied sheets, and satchel were recoverable; a separate Watcher Two escaped. The boatman in the skiff had been left alive but in grave need of aid.
The group paid 10 gp at an Ilmatari dockside shrine for a healing spell for Pip, rather than assuming every injury was resolved for free.
On Day 10, Vesper scouted the Old Chain Baths: no clear rooftop lookout, but a recently fitted rope ladder connecting the upper ruin to a lower service area.
The Old Chain Baths' lower occupants included the sling-woman, a slight adolescent courier, and an older, lean coordinator in dock clothes. The lower site had both an eastern drain exit and a deeper cistern/service route.
At that site, Pip concealed himself near the deeper exit while Gaspar and Edric approached from the eastern drain side. Gaspar's Ray of Frost slowed the older man enough for Pip to stop his flight.
The older man was knocked out alive, and the narrow case he carried fell at the choke point rather than being lost in the passage.
On Day 11, a suspicious older visitor entered the public gate of the North Tally complex. Pip's tail remained clean; Vesper lost sight of him under covered structures. Do not invent which inner building he entered.
Vesper later confirmed that the missing gray calfskin correction ledger was on a worktable in a closed counting-house room.
Marris Pell, nervous and working alone, was using a knife, scraper, replacement folios, sealing wax, and an oil lamp to alter selected ledger entries; he was not merely about to throw the entire book into a fire.
The counting-house room had one usable door and a barred, too-small clerestory; a thin watcher stood outside by the archival-copying office.
The corrected gray ledger, alteration evidence, and Marris's live capture linked the Whitewater line to manipulation of official North Tally records.
Mavros expedition and return-road odds and ends (Days 12–14)
The party received sealed customs credentials from Deputy Tallymaster Orsana Mere before riding north to investigate Deputy Road Surveyor Aldren Mavros.
Mavros's survey carriage was found off the main road in a screened drainage hollow south of the North Road Survey Depot. It would not have been obvious from the normal road approach.
Depot rider Lysa Tor became an active helper. The trio initially left the cart, both horses, Nib, and Ripper at the depot to avoid disturbing the carriage scene; Vesper accompanied the search.
The depot counted as a Safe Long Rest because of its secure conditions, despite the campaign's more restrictive wilderness-rest system.
At the abandoned chain hut, Vesper discovered a hidden mule in a rear lean-to, a crude alarm cord along the approach, and a rear shutter/escape gap.
Mavros was found alive but bound, injured, and exhausted. One captor reviewed papers while a heavier guard watched the exits.
Mavros identified the paperwork-oriented captor as Calvern Rusk. The second, heavier captor has no established personal name; don't supply one retroactively.
The retrieved papers and materials included a modified chain, forged authorization, route documents, wax/stamp supplies, and a transfer note. These are evidence, not souvenirs.
On the return road, a deliberately disabled handcart served as bait for an ambush. A watcher in the brush carried a horn for signaling.
Pip's hidden arrows struck the brush watcher before he could sound the horn; another arrow brought down the first roadman. Because these were ranged attacks, their victims needed stabilization, not a retroactive declaration of nonlethal sword damage.
The second roadman had thrown a knife and wounded Lucero before an arrow from Lysa and a small Fire Bolt from Edric shook his resolve. He ultimately surrendered.
The party repaired the attackers' sabotaged handcart rather than abandoning wounded enemies along the road.
The wounded first roadman and brush watcher were stabilized and loaded onto that handcart. The surviving second roadman was tied to it by a waistcord and made to help pull it toward Zazesspur.
A charcoal-ticked road cloth indicated an additional/crossed-out southern position, suggesting a runner or fallback signaling point was still in play.
Gaspar rode Lucero to catch the south runner at a shepherd-track cut-off, then knocked him unconscious with the flat of the ancestral sword, leaving the runner and his message tube intact.
The road messages included "Delay road party if witness rides" and "Mark escort strength" — practical proof that someone wanted the party slowed and counted.
The party deliberately split the most sensitive evidence among different carriers during the return journey rather than trusting a single vulnerable satchel.
The returning group delivered six living captives: Calvern Rusk, his unnamed heavy guard, roadman one, roadman two, the brush watcher, and the south runner.
Mere received Mavros alive along with the route-fraud documentation, altered equipment, and runner's message tube. All the delivered evidence and captives are now in official custody, not still in the cart.

NPC mannerisms, hooks, and tone to preserve
Deputy Tallymaster Orsana Mere takes custody and chain-of-evidence issues seriously and differentiates between the merits of a rescue, evidence collection, and captured suspects.
Orven Tal is a good model for dry, correct, only mildly impressed officialdom. Characters need not react to the party with heroic awe every time.
Maerla Sorn is capable and wary, including of the possibility of planted scouts among legitimate-looking travelers; her discretion can feel ethically ambiguous.
Ilvarren Thale can be interested in a story, wine, and competent curiosity rather than merely gold. His tutelage was a relationship as much as a mechanical benefit.
Halda Veyr and The Low Arch are recurring anchor points in a city where the party is still comparatively new.
Pip and Nib can make camp or inn scenes interesting through practical arrangements: wolf accommodation, weapon storage, bargaining, and games.
The Fox-and-Geese game is a small way to keep the camp feeling inhabited without another combat or exposition scene.
The escaped Drowned Heron Watcher Two, the veiled woman, and higher Whitewater coordinators should not be silently marked captured just because many other conspirators fell alive.

Continuity guardrails
The campaign uses the 2024 D&D rules as adapted at the table, with milestone-led advancement and individual character records. Never let a rough XP tracking note override a later confirmed level.
Proposed level-up spells, features, feats, and discretionary choices require player review before becoming finalized; existing confirmed level-4 decisions are established.
In unsafe wilderness, ruins, and contested roads, overnight sleep gives the restricted Excursion Rest, not an unrestricted Long Rest. Secure inns and properly garrisoned places can permit a Safe Long Rest.
Keep names conventionally Tethyrian in the region unless a person has a reason for a different naming tradition. The Hispanic flavor of Espada/Valespada is deliberate family-specific flavor, not a rule for every NPC in Tethyr.
When a state detail is unknown (arrows, current horse injuries, exact storage location of a released knife or coat), mark it unverified rather than inventing a convenient value.
Append newly discovered small facts to the relevant section, and change Open to Resolved or move completed promises to their proper historical section when the campaign advances.
