# Azure Banner — Day 14: Ambushes on the Return Road

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 14  
**Location:** North Road Survey Depot–Zazesspur return route; The Low Arch, Zazesspur

## Chronological log

- **Returning with proof and a witness:** **Gaspar, Pip, Edric, Lysa Tor, and the traveling company** set out from the depot toward Zazesspur with **Aldren Mavros**, two bound prisoners (**Calvern Rusk** and his unnamed heavier guard), altered surveying equipment, forgery records, and other documentation. They divided sensitive evidence among carriers and kept a wary lookout for coordinated interference.
- **A staged obstruction:** Roadfolk posed beside a deliberately disabled **handcart** while a concealed **brush watcher** with a horn waited to signal. The party recognized the danger. Gaspar continued trying to get their own cart and vulnerable passengers past the obstruction; **Pip** hid and watched the signaling position while **Edric and Lysa** provided covering support.
- **Pip stops the signal:** Pip struck the hidden horn watcher with an arrow before the horn could sound, then hit him again, leaving him critically wounded. The trap's roadmen turned hostile. Another roadman suffered a hidden arrow from Pip and fell critically wounded. **Ranged attacks were not retroactively nonlethal**; immediate stabilization was required to keep the fallen attackers alive.
- **Lucero wounded, surrender accepted:** The other roadman threw a knife that **wounded Gaspar's riding horse Lucero**. **Lysa's arrow** and **Edric's Fire Bolt** injured the attacker; he surrendered rather than fight to the death. The party accepted the surrender and took care of the wounded.
- **A useful, awkward prisoner wagon:** Instead of leaving disabled enemies, the companions **repaired the sabotaged handcart**, loaded the stabilized horn watcher and first roadman onto it, and tied the surrendered second roadman to it by a waistcord to pull it along under guard.
- **The south-position clue:** A **charcoal-ticked road cloth** revealed another marked (and crossed-out) position toward the south. The party continued cautiously and sought the associated **runner** before a warning could reach Whitewater.
- **Gaspar's mounted intercept:** Riding **Lucero**, Gaspar cut off the south runner at a shepherd-track approach, struck him with the **flat of the ancestral sword**, and subdued him **alive**. **Lysa** helped secure him and his undestroyed **message tube**. Road orders included **“Delay road party if witness rides”** and **“Mark escort strength”**, demonstrating a plan to slow or assess the escort.
- **Return to official custody:** The party reached **Zazesspur** and delivered **Mavros alive**, the altered chain and documents, and **six living captives**: Rusk, the unnamed guard, the two handcart roadmen, the horn watcher, and the south runner. **Deputy Tallymaster Orsana Mere** received the evidence and arranged formal custody. The party returned to **The Low Arch**.
- **An escalated summons:** Because the case now reached beyond one deputy's authority, the party was **summoned for the following morning** to the **Hall of Measures** to meet a higher Crown-appointed official. The meeting itself has **not yet occurred** at the Day 14 endpoint.
