# Azure Banner — Day 09: The Stolen Customs Ledger

**In-world day label:** Zazesspur Day 9  
**Location:** Zazesspur Lower City, fish-stairs, sail-menders' row, and the Drowned Heron

## Chronological log

- **A coordinated theft:** Customs worker **Taman Rill** was robbed of an official ledger when a rehearsed team distracted him with a fallen basket and cut his satchel. **Pip**, with **Edric** assisting, reconstructed a red-scarfed man directing or screening the theft, a small right-handed cutter at the victim's left hip, and a likely lookout. Black waxed thread carried the distinctive smell of **clove oil and chalk/lime**, connected to Whitewater's marks.
- **Tracing the thieves:** Inquiries at **Marra's broth stall** and the **lower fish-stairs** located frightened young **Noll**. Pip persuaded him not to flee and promised protection. **Gaspar** detected a listener above the stairs: **Brant**, identifiable by a rust-red scarf, dark beard, gold tooth, and clove scent, with an accomplice nearby.
- **Confrontation in sail-menders' row:** The pursuit led to a fight. **Miv**, the red-haired young cutter, escaped Brant's grasp and came back toward the party. **Edric** advanced under cover, using *Fire Bolt*, while Gaspar pressed the wounded Brant. **Brant was subdued and stabilized**, and the authorities took him into custody; Noll was placed under protection. The trio sought to reassure Miv that Noll was safe before asking about her part in the theft and the unidentified veiled woman.
- **The Drowned Heron trail:** The ledger had been taken to a guarded copying site. **Pip** infiltrated the neighboring roof while **Edric** covered it from above with help from familiar **Vesper**. Pip sabotaged a rooftop **signal cord**; its first pull jammed, delaying the alarm as the suspects tried to escape with the original book.
- **A failed bluff, then a rescue of the evidence:** Pip tried *Minor Illusion* in Brant's voice to hold up the handler, but the deception **failed**. Combat followed; Edric's fire and Pip's melee work overwhelmed the defenders. Pip knocked a burned rear crossbow guard **unconscious alive**. The **handler, copyist, rooftop watcher, one watcher, and rear guard** were all down alive; a **second watcher / sling-woman escaped**. A skiff boatman survived the encounter but needed urgent aid.
- **Evidence recovered:** The **original customs ledger**, copied sheets, and satchel were secured for customs rather than kept as loot. The Whitewater network was implicated, but the **escaped operative, veiled woman, 'W', 'Weigher', and 'Bell before green'** remained unresolved.
- **Payment and rest:** Customs paid the full **60 gp** ledger-job reward. The group donated **10 gp** at an Ilmatari dockside shrine for healing on Pip, then rested at The Low Arch. Nib, Vesper, and Gaspar shared watches so Pip and Edric could sleep undisturbed.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Shared purse:** 381 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp before reward; **441 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** after payment; **431 gp, 4 sp, 5 cp** after shrine healing.
- **Safety and recovery:** Pip was healed from 12/21 to 21/21 by the shrine. A Safe Long Rest refreshed the party's usual rest resources; Edric and Gaspar recovered from the day's injuries.
- **Open leads:** Captured people do not include the escaped second watcher / sling-woman; the veiled woman and wider Whitewater organization remain active threats.
