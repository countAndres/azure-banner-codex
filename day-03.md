# Azure Banner — Day 03: Looking for Work in Darromar

**In-world day:** 3  
**Location:** Darromar, including the Royal Quarter, Red Spur, Southbank Watch, and camp outside the city

## Chronological log

- **Chasing the lead:** After the night at Three Reeds, **Mariela Tann** helped identify Tomas Rell and the Aldana Merchant Coster connection. The trio sought an opportunity substantial enough to support their travels.
- **Gaspar's Royal Quarter attempt:** Gaspar insisted on trying the respectable, high-status districts despite Pip's doubts. An approach at the **Crowned Heron** in the Royal Quarter was dismissive; what was offered amounted to humiliating menial labor for a few copper pieces, not the honorable work Gaspar hoped to find.
- **Pip's practical search:** Pip led inquiries around the **Red Spur**, but the available jobs were unsuitable or unappealing. The companions declined them rather than taking work merely to stay busy.
- **Edric's curiosity:** The **Lantern House** drew attention as a possible arcane connection, but Edric postponed pursuing it in favor of the immediate work search.
- **A real commission:** At the Southbank Watch, **Corporal Ysabel Marrón** confirmed that the authorities had heard the Corza report but lacked the capacity to investigate it promptly. The offered compensation was **8 silver pieces, with meals/lodging** associated with the farm. She gave directions via the north road and a **broken red-wheel shrine**, then roughly a mile east toward Corza's place.
- **Next move:** The trio accepted the Corza investigation as a worthwhile small job and camped outside Darromar rather than spending another night seeking prestige in the city.

## End-of-day state / continuity

- **Active lead:** Missing sheep, an injured/eaten ewe, and unusual blue lights near an abandoned tollhouse.
- **Relationship notes:** Gaspar experienced a check to his noble expectations; Pip's financial realism proved valuable; Marrón became an important Watch contact.
- The **actual Corza farm visit and tollhouse discovery are Day 4** events.
